论霍奇猜想

Burt Totaro 2026-09-11

[This is a guest post by Burt Totaro. This blog post was initially written in a different file format and converted using AI. — T.]

These are strange times for mathematicians. It now seems possible that AI companies will burn through vast resources in order to prove some new fact about the Hodge conjecture. I’d like to discuss the current status of the Hodge conjecture, in order to think about the value to the mathematical community of exploring such hard problems. Thanks to Terry Tao for suggesting this guest post.

The Hodge conjecture crystallizes a big mystery: the relation between topology and algebraic geometry, or (what amounts to the same) between real and complex geometry. In short, real submanifolds are flexible, whereas complex submanifolds are quite rigid, and it is a challenge to relate the two. The setting is a “smooth complex projective variety” X{X}X, a complex manifold defined by algebraic equations. We want to understand the possible shapes of complex algebraic subspaces of X{X}X. (By a famous result of Wei-Liang Chow (1949), every complex analytic submanifold of X{X}X is in fact algebraic.) One could ask whether every real submanifold of X{X}X can be moved continuously to a complex submanifold. That is far too optimistic. Still, to a first approximation, the Hodge conjecture predicts which real submanifolds can be moved continuously to a complex submanifold, in terms of whether certain integrals are zero. (More precisely: every Hodge class in the rational cohomology of X{X}X should be the class of an algebraic cycle.) The conjecture goes back to William Hodge (1950).

Now suppose in some counterfactual world that the day after the conjecture was made some inhuman oracle told us that the answer was “yes” and the conjecture was considered “solved.” We can see, by examining our real world, the vast body of mathematical knowledge that would have been lost. In our world, what we want from a great conjecture is a challenge that inspires all kinds of other discoveries, even if the original question remains open. The Hodge conjecture has been that kind of conjecture for geometers.

A key piece of evidence for the Hodge conjecture is the “ Lefschetz (1,1){(1,1)}(1,1) -theorem “, which says that the Hodge conjecture is true for algebraic cycles of codimension 1 (that is, of complex dimension n−1{n-1}n−1, if X{X}X has complex dimension n{n}n), and also for cycles of dimension 1. Solomon Lefschetz’s work was quite early (around 1924), well before the Hodge conjecture was stated in general. Interestingly, some of the most important proofs by both Lefschetz and Hodge have gaps, from our current perspective. They had tremendous insight, but the full justification of their results required hard analysis by many mathematicians, notably Kunihiko Kodaira (around 1950).

By some measures, one could say that progress on the Hodge conjecture has been limited. In view of Lefschetz’s results, the first open case is for codimension-2 cycles on a variety of complex dimension 4, and that case (for arbitrary varieties of dimension 4) still seems far out of reach. But the Hodge conjecture has been enormously successful for inspiring new mathematical theories, such as Phillip Griffiths’s “ Variations of Hodge structure ” (starting in the 1960s) and Pierre Deligne’s “ absolute Hodge cycles “. Broadly speaking, the Hodge conjecture suggests that the structure of algebraic cycles should be controlled by Hodge theory, in other words by integrals of algebraic functions. This hope has led to vast numbers of proved insights about the structure of algebraic cycles. (For example, Griffiths disproved Grothendieck’s conjecture that algebraic and homological equivalence were the same, which was a surprise; but he used Hodge theory in order to do it.) In recent decades, Claire Voisin has been a leader in using Hodge theory in new ways to get information about algebraic cycles.

An exciting recent development is Eyal Markman’s 2025 proof of the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties of dimension 4 and 5. (Abelian varieties, the tori with a complex algebraic structure, are very special compared to all algebraic varieties; but they are a particularly important class in many ways, for example in number theory.) This is a monumental piece of work that builds on many earlier developments. André Weil (1977) identified a class of abelian varieties, those of Weil type, for which there are “unexpected” Hodge classes that do not appear on most other abelian varieties. In low dimensions such as 4, Ben Moonen and Yuri Zarhin (1995) showed that the Hodge conjecture for all abelian varieties would follow from the special case of abelian varieties of Weil type. Spencer Bloch (1972), extended by Ragnar-Olaf Buchweitz and Hubert Flenner (2003), defined a property called “semi-regularity” which, when it holds, allows proving the Hodge conjecture for a continuous family of varieties when it holds for one variety in the family.

For a long time, however, semi-regularity seemed far too strong a condition ever to apply to hard cases of the Hodge conjecture. Markman found, with great ingenuity, how to construct enough examples of semi-regular sheaves to prove the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties in dimensions 4 and 5. His ideas use several big theories that have been developed in recent decades, notably about derived equivalences between algebraic varieties and about hyperkähler manifolds. In the end, Markman actually needed an extension of Buchweitz–Flenner’s semi-regularity theorem to “twisted sheaves”, which was supplied by Jonathan Pridham (2024).

At this point, I am confident that with enough effort, it will be possible to push these ideas further. As we have seen, AI companies are willing to mobilize resources on an incomprehensible scale to attack problems that attract their interest. We don’t know how much insight may come from any given AI-generated proof. But there is reason to worry about the commercial pressure on AI companies to claim advances at high speed and the knock-on effect this has on mathematicians. The power of mathematical ideas comes from a continual negotiation among people. What we care about are not so much isolated facts, but rather the ideas and inspiration that the search leads to.

对数学家而言，这是一个奇异的时代。如今看来，AI 公司似乎有可能耗费巨量资源，来证明关于霍奇猜想的某个新事实。我想谈谈霍奇猜想的当前状况，借此思考：探索这样的难题，对数学共同体究竟有什么价值。感谢陶哲轩提议我写这篇客座文章。

霍奇猜想凝聚了一个重大的谜团：拓扑与代数几何之间的关系，或者说，实几何与复几何之间的关系，两种说法在这里是一回事。简而言之，实子流形是灵活的，而复子流形相当刚性，如何把两者联系起来是一个挑战。我们讨论的对象是一个“光滑复射影簇” X{X}X，也就是由代数方程定义的复流形。我们希望理解 X{X}X 的复代数子空间可能具有哪些形状。（根据周炜良在 1949 年得到的一个著名结果，X{X}X 的每个复解析子流形实际上都是代数的。）人们可以问：X{X}X 的每个实子流形，是否都能连续变形为一个复子流形？这种期待过于乐观。不过，粗略地说，霍奇猜想根据某些积分是否为零，预言哪些实子流形能够连续变形为复子流形。（更准确地说：X{X}X 的有理上同调中的每个霍奇类，都应当是某个代数闭链的类。）这一猜想可以追溯到威廉·霍奇 1950 年的工作。

现在，设想在一个与现实不同的世界里，猜想提出后的第二天，某个非人类的神谕便告诉我们，答案是“是”，于是这个猜想就被视为已经“解决”。考察我们真实的世界，就能看出那样会失去多么浩瀚的数学知识。在我们的世界里，我们希望一个伟大的猜想带来一种挑战，激发各种各样的其他发现，即使最初的问题仍未解决。对于几何学家来说，霍奇猜想正是这样的猜想。

支持霍奇猜想的一项关键证据是“莱夫谢茨 (1,1){(1,1)}(1,1) 定理”。它表明，霍奇猜想对于余维为 1 的代数闭链成立（也就是说，如果 X{X}X 的复维数是 n{n}n，这些闭链的复维数就是 n−1{n-1}n−1），对于维数为 1 的闭链也成立。所罗门·莱夫谢茨的这项工作相当早，大约完成于 1924 年，远早于霍奇猜想的一般表述。有意思的是，从今天的角度看，莱夫谢茨和霍奇各自一些最重要的证明都存在缺漏。他们有非凡的洞见，但要为其结果给出完整论证，还需要许多数学家运用艰深的分析方法，其中尤其值得一提的是小平邦彦在 1950 年前后的工作。

按照某些衡量标准，可以说霍奇猜想的进展有限。鉴于莱夫谢茨的结果，第一个尚未解决的情形，是复维数为 4 的簇上的余维 2 闭链；而这一情形，对任意 4 维簇而言，似乎仍然遥不可及。但是，霍奇猜想在激发新的数学理论方面取得了巨大成功，例如菲利普·格里菲思从 20 世纪 60 年代开始发展的“霍奇结构变分”，以及皮埃尔·德利涅的“绝对霍奇闭链”。大体而言，霍奇猜想暗示，代数闭链的结构应当由霍奇理论来支配，换言之，由代数函数的积分来支配。这种期待带来了大量关于代数闭链结构、并已得到证明的深刻认识。（例如，格里菲思推翻了格罗滕迪克关于代数等价与同调等价相同的猜想，这出人意料；但他正是利用霍奇理论做到的。）近几十年来，克莱尔·瓦赞一直是以新方式运用霍奇理论、获取代数闭链信息的领军人物。

近期一项令人振奋的进展，是 Eyal Markman 于 2025 年证明了 4 维和 5 维阿贝尔簇的霍奇猜想。（阿贝尔簇是具有复代数结构的环面；在所有代数簇中，它们非常特殊，但在许多方面，例如数论中，又是格外重要的一类。）这是一项建立在众多早期进展之上的里程碑式工作。安德烈·韦伊在 1977 年辨识出一类阿贝尔簇，即韦伊型阿贝尔簇：它们具有一些“意想不到的”霍奇类，而这些类在大多数其他阿贝尔簇上并不出现。在 4 维这样的低维情形，Ben Moonen 和 Yuri Zarhin 于 1995 年证明，所有阿贝尔簇的霍奇猜想都可以归结为韦伊型阿贝尔簇这一特殊情形。Spencer Bloch 于 1972 年定义了一种称为“半正则性”的性质，Ragnar-Olaf Buchweitz 和 Hubert Flenner 于 2003 年对它作了推广。当这一性质成立时，只要一个连续簇族中的某个簇满足霍奇猜想，就能证明整个簇族都满足霍奇猜想。

然而，很长一段时间里，半正则性似乎是一个过强的条件，根本无法应用于霍奇猜想的困难情形。Markman 极其巧妙地找到了构造足够多半正则层的方法，从而证明了 4 维和 5 维阿贝尔簇的霍奇猜想。他的思路运用了近几十年发展起来的若干庞大理论，尤其是关于代数簇之间的导出等价，以及超凯勒流形的理论。最终，Markman 实际上还需要将 Buchweitz–Flenner 的半正则性定理推广到“扭曲层”；Jonathan Pridham 于 2024 年提供了这一推广。

至此，我确信，只要投入足够的努力，就能够进一步推进这些思路。正如我们所见，AI 公司愿意调动规模之大令人难以理解的资源，去攻克吸引其兴趣的问题。我们不知道某个 AI 生成的证明能够带来多少洞见。但是，我们有理由担忧：商业压力促使 AI 公司高速宣称进展，而这又会给数学家带来连锁影响。数学思想的力量，来自人们之间持续不断的交流与协商。我们关心的与其说是孤立的事实，不如说是探索过程带来的思想与启发。
